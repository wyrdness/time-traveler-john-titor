## Overview

John Titor is the screen name attached to a series of online posts claiming time-traveler status that appeared on the Art Bell Post-2-Post BBS, Time Travel Institute forums, and IRC channels between 1998 and late March 2001. The earliest known communications were two faxes sent to broadcaster Art Bell on 29 July 1998. The poster claimed to be a US Army soldier born in 1998 and based in Tampa, Florida in 2036, sent back to 1975 to retrieve an IBM 5100 computer needed to debug legacy systems threatened by the year-2038 Unix problem ('Y2038'); he claimed to be 'on a stopover' in 2000-2001 to visit his family. He posted detailed schematics, photographs of an alleged 'time displacement device' built into a 1967 Chevrolet Corvette and 1987 truck, and a series of predictions including civil unrest around the 2004 US presidential election, a US civil war by 2008, and a global nuclear war ('N-Day') in 2015 with the new US capital relocated to Omaha, Nebraska. None of the predictions came to pass on schedule. A 2009 investigation by John Hughston ('Razimus') and Italian researchers identified Larry Haber, a Florida entertainment lawyer who became CEO of the John Titor Foundation (incorporated 2003-09-16), as the most likely originator, alongside his brothers Morey Haber and John Rick Haber - all of whom have denied involvement. The Titor archive remains one of the most enduring artifacts of late-1990s/early-2000s internet folklore.

## Theories & Explanations

### Paranormal / Cryptozoological

**Genuine time traveler from 2036** _(Titor (anonymous poster))_

Titor's own claim: an American soldier sent back from 2036 via a micro-singularity-based time-displacement unit to retrieve an IBM 5100 computer for the Y2038 problem.

**Alternate-history Titor** _(Various Titor enthusiasts)_

A defense sometimes raised after predictions failed: Titor came from a divergent timeline and his future is not ours.

### Skeptical

**Larry Haber / Haber brothers hoax** _(John Hughston (Razimus), Brian Dunning (Skeptoid), Italian RAI documentary investigation)_

The most credible attribution: Florida entertainment lawyer Larry Haber, who became CEO of the John Titor Foundation Inc. (incorporated 16 September 2003), and/or his computer-professional brothers Morey Haber and John Rick Haber, originated the Titor posts. All deny involvement.

**Falsified predictions** _(Public record)_

Titor's central predictions - civil unrest in 2004, US civil war by 2008, global nuclear war ('N-Day') in 2015 - did not occur.

**Internet-folklore hoax tradition** _(Joe Nickell, Various internet-folklore researchers)_

Titor fits a recognizable pattern of late-1990s/early-2000s internet hoaxes (alongside earlier and later examples), exploiting the credibility of forum culture and Art Bell's audience.
